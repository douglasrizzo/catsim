Extending catsim#

The initializer, the selector, the estimator and the stopper of a test, and the engine’s optional exposure control, are strategies: objects that CatEngine calls at fixed points of every session. A method catsim lacks is a subclass of a strategy’s base class, and it runs in live sessions and in SimulationRunner like the built-in ones, with their metrics and plots. This guide is for method authors: it lists what each strategy receives and returns and the rules every strategy keeps, then writes a custom selector and a custom stopper and runs each in a simulation. It assumes Running Simulations.

The exposure control is optional: an engine has one only when it is given one, as CatEngine(..., exposure_control=...). An item it declines is excluded from the session for the rest of its test, and the selector proposes again. Live CAT Sessions shows one at work.

Decorate each method you override with @override, from the typing_extensions package that catsim depends on, so that a type checker reports a method that overrides nothing, such as one with a misspelt name. Annotate arrays with the aliases of catsim.typing, FloatArray and IntArray, as catsim’s own signatures do.

What a Strategy Reads#

The initializer runs before the session has any state, so it receives only the item bank and the run’s random generator. The other strategies receive a StepContext, which the engine builds for every call:

Field

What it holds

item_bank

the session’s ItemBank

rng

the run’s random generator

administered_items, responses

the items given so far and the responses to them, in order, as tuples

excluded_items

items the selector must not choose although they were not given, such as those the exposure control declined

theta, se

the current ability estimate and its standard error

exposure_rates

each item’s administrations divided by the other sessions started, or None when the run counts no exposure, in which case no cap applies; an item this session was given counts its own administration, so its rate can exceed 1

The selector receives the session as it stands, and the exposure control the same context with the items it already declined in this selection among the excluded ones. The estimator receives the newest item and response, with theta and se still those of the previous estimate, its starting point. The stopper receives the new estimate as well; before the first item, it receives the initial estimate, no items and an infinite se, so it must handle an empty test. No context holds the examinee’s true ability, so a strategy cannot see the simulated truth.

An estimator returns an Estimate: theta, which must be finite; se, its standard error by the estimator’s own formula, which may be math.inf but never NaN, because the precision stoppers compare it with their targets; and evaluations, how many times the estimator evaluated its objective, or None. For the likelihood, catsim.irt.log_likelihood_function() returns the log-likelihood of the responses as a function of the ability, and catsim.irt.log_response_probabilities() returns \(\log P_i(\theta)\) and \(\log Q_i(\theta)\), exact even far from the item’s difficulty. Bayesian estimators can build on the priors and the posterior of Bayesian Estimation Infrastructure.

The Rules a Strategy Keeps#

  • No per-session state. A strategy holds its configuration and caches derived from the item bank, nothing else. One strategy object serves every session of a run, an application may interleave the sessions of many examinees on one engine, and look-ahead selectors such as MEI call their estimator with hypothetical responses. Whatever a strategy needs about the session, it computes from the context at each call.

  • Randomness only from the run’s generator: context.rng, or the initializer’s rng. Every random draw of a run then comes from one generator, so a seeded run repeats (Reproducibility). A strategy that draws from anywhere else, such as a generator of its own or numpy.random’s global functions, escapes the run’s seed.

  • A selector chooses an item neither administered nor excluded, and raises NoItemsAvailableError when it has none to offer; the engine then ends the session with StopReason.NO_ITEMS. A selector that passes a changed context to another, with dataclasses.replace(), adds to its excluded items and never removes any.

  • A stopper is pure: the same context always gives the same answer, so a StopRule can be asked after a run which of its stoppers held.

  • An estimator’s estimate is finite once an item has been administered, whatever the responses, all correct and all incorrect included.

ADR-0005: Strategies Take One Step Context and Estimators Return an Estimate explains why the strategies are shaped this way.

The Selector Helpers#

catsim.selection exports the functions that make the built-in selectors behave alike. A custom selector that uses them draws its candidates, applies an exposure cap and breaks ties as the built-in ones do:

  • remaining() returns the items neither administered nor excluded, and raises NoItemsAvailableError when there are none;

  • below_cap() keeps the candidates whose exposure rate is below a cap r_max, or all of them when none is;

  • pick_best() returns the candidate with the highest score, and breaks exact ties at random with the generator it is given, as every built-in selector does (Selection Techniques);

  • best_pool() returns the best candidates, with every candidate tied at the cutoff, for selectors that draw from a pool of good items;

  • check_r_max() and check_test_size() check a selector’s constructor arguments.

A Custom Selector#

Content balancing keeps each test’s mix of content areas, such as algebra, geometry and statistics, close to a target. The selector below gives the most informative item of the area given least so far in the session, among the items under an exposure cap. The areas, one per item, and their number are configuration; how many items of each area the session has had is computed from the context at every call, never stored.

import numpy
from typing_extensions import override

from catsim import CatEngine, ItemBank, NoItemsAvailableError, SimulationRunner, StepContext
from catsim.estimation import EAPEstimator
from catsim.initialization import FixedPointInitializer
from catsim.selection import BaseSelector, MaxInfoSelector, below_cap, check_r_max, pick_best, remaining
from catsim.stopping import BaseStopper, MaxLengthStopper, StopRule
from catsim.typing import IntArray


class ContentBalancedSelector(BaseSelector):
    """Give the most informative item of the content area given least so far."""

    def __init__(self, areas: IntArray, r_max: float = 1.0) -> None:
        self.areas = numpy.asarray(areas)
        self.n_areas = int(numpy.max(self.areas)) + 1
        self.r_max = check_r_max(r_max)

    @override
    def select(self, context: StepContext) -> int:
        candidates = remaining(context)
        given = numpy.bincount(self.areas[list(context.administered_items)], minlength=self.n_areas)
        open_areas = numpy.unique(self.areas[candidates])
        area = open_areas[numpy.argmin(given[open_areas])]
        in_area = below_cap(candidates[self.areas[candidates] == area], context.exposure_rates, self.r_max)
        return pick_best(in_area, context.item_bank.information(context.theta)[in_area], context.rng)

Among the areas with items left, argmin picks the one given least, the lowest-numbered on a tie. A strategy can be called by hand, with a context built from its two required fields and any others, which is how to test one:

item_bank = ItemBank.generate(300, "3PL", rng=1)
areas = numpy.resize([0, 1, 2], item_bank.n_items)
selector = ContentBalancedSelector(areas, r_max=0.3)
context = StepContext(item_bank=item_bank, rng=numpy.random.default_rng(0), administered_items=(0, 1), responses=(True, False))
item_id = selector.select(context)
print(item_id, areas[item_id])

The items belong to three areas in turn: 0, 1, 2, 0, 1, 2 and so on. Items 0 and 1 are of areas 0 and 1, so the selector gives an item of area 2. In a simulation, the selector goes wherever a built-in one does:

true_thetas = numpy.random.default_rng(2).normal(0, 1, 500)
engine = CatEngine(item_bank, FixedPointInitializer(0.0), selector, EAPEstimator(), MaxLengthStopper(12))
result = SimulationRunner(engine).run(true_thetas, rng=3)
print(areas[result.sessions[0].administered_items])
print(f"RMSE {result.rmse:.3f}, highest exposure rate {numpy.max(result.exposure_rates):.2f}")

Every test of 12 items gives 4 of each area, in turn, and no item’s exposure rate goes above the cap of 0.3.

A Custom Stopper#

A test can end when no item left in the bank would tell much more about the examinee. The stopper below ends the test once the most informative remaining item gives less than min_info at the current estimate. It reads the item bank, the estimate and the remaining items from the context, so it is pure.

class MinInfoStopper(BaseStopper):
    """Stop once no remaining item gives at least `min_info` at the current estimate."""

    def __init__(self, min_info: float) -> None:
        self.min_info = min_info

    @override
    def stop(self, context: StepContext) -> bool:
        try:
            candidates = remaining(context)
        except NoItemsAvailableError:
            return True
        return bool(numpy.max(context.item_bank.information(context.theta)[candidates]) < self.min_info)


stopper = StopRule(MinInfoStopper(0.2), MaxLengthStopper(30), min_items=5)
engine = CatEngine(item_bank, FixedPointInitializer(0.0), MaxInfoSelector(), EAPEstimator(), stopper)
result = SimulationRunner(engine).run(true_thetas, rng=3)
early = result.test_lengths < 30
extreme = numpy.abs(true_thetas) > 1.5
print(f"{numpy.sum(early)} tests end before 30 items")
print(f"abilities beyond ±1.5: {numpy.mean(extreme):.2f} of the population, {numpy.mean(extreme[early]):.2f} of those tests")

A StopRule combines the custom stopper with a length cap and a minimum length, as it does the built-in ones. In this run, 33 of the 500 tests end before the cap, nearly 9 in 10 of them for examinees whose ability lies beyond ±1.5, against 14% of the population: the bank has few informative items at those abilities, and the stopper ends the test once it has given them.
