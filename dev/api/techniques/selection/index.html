Selection Techniques#

Item selection techniques determine which item from the bank to administer at each step of an adaptive test. The choice of selector affects both measurement precision and practical properties such as item exposure and content coverage.

Implemented#

  • Linear Selector
  • Random Selector
  • Randomesque Selector
  • 54321 Selector
  • Maximum Fisher Information Selector
  • Urry Selector
  • Interval Information Selector
  • Cluster Selector
  • Stratified Selector
  • A-Stratified Selector
  • A-Stratified with B-Blocking Selector
  • Maximum-Information Stratified Selector
  • Maximum-Information with B-Blocking Selector
