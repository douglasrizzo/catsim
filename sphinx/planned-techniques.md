# Planned techniques

The methods below are not yet available in catsim; each one's specification on GitHub gives its
definition, parameters and sources.

| Technique | Family | Motivation | Key reference | Spec |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Owen sequential Bayesian estimator | estimation | Reproduces Owen's procedure, which carries only the mean and variance of a normal approximation of the posterior from one response to the next, and shows how that approximation and the order of the responses move the ability estimate away from the full posterior's estimate | {cite:p}`Owen1975` | [owen-estimator.md](https://github.com/douglasrizzo/catsim/blob/dev/specs/estimation/owen-estimator.md) |
| Posterior Kullback-Leibler selector (KLP) | selection | Weights the KL divergence by the current posterior, not by the {doc}`KL selector's </api/techniques/selection/kl-selector>` uniform window, so selection is broad early in the test and concentrates as the posterior narrows, with no half-width schedule to tune | {cite:p}`Chang1996` | [klp-selector.md](https://github.com/douglasrizzo/catsim/blob/dev/specs/selection/klp-selector.md) |
| Minimum expected posterior variance selector (MEPV) | selection | Chooses the item that minimizes the expected posterior variance after the next response, which targets squared-error loss directly; its gains over {doc}`MEI </api/techniques/selection/mei-selector>` and MPWI are modest | {cite:p}`vanderLinden1998`; {cite:p}`Choi2009` | [mepv-selector.md](https://github.com/douglasrizzo/catsim/blob/dev/specs/selection/mepv-selector.md) |
| Maximum posterior weighted information selector (MPWI) | selection | Weights each item's information by the current posterior: the Bayesian counterpart of {doc}`MLWI </api/techniques/selection/mlwi-selector>`, whose prior can carry what is known about the examinee in advance | {cite:p}`vanderLinden1998` | [mpwi-selector.md](https://github.com/douglasrizzo/catsim/blob/dev/specs/selection/mpwi-selector.md) |
| Sympson-Hetter exposure control | exposure control | Accepts the item the selector proposes with a probability calibrated by simulation, so that each item's exposure rate stays at about the exposure cap $r_{\max}$ or below; the mechanism works with any selector, but its probabilities are calibrated for one selector, test design and population | {cite:p}`Sympson1985`; {cite:p}`Stocking1998` | [sympson-hetter-exposure.md](https://github.com/douglasrizzo/catsim/blob/dev/specs/selection/sympson-hetter-exposure.md) |
| Sequential probability ratio test stopper (SPRT) | stopping | Classifies the examinee above or below a cut score as soon as the likelihood ratio of the responses at two abilities around the cut crosses Wald's bounds, deciding from the likelihood where the {doc}`confidence-interval stopper </api/techniques/stopping/confidence-interval-stopper>` decides from the ability estimate and its standard error | {cite:p}`Reckase1983`; {cite:p}`Wald1947` | [sprt-stopper.md](https://github.com/douglasrizzo/catsim/blob/dev/specs/stopping/sprt-stopper.md) |
| Generalized likelihood ratio stopper (GLR) | stopping | Compares the likelihood at the maximum likelihood estimate with that at the nearest point of the competing hypothesis, not at the SPRT's two fixed points; in Bartroff et al.'s simulations, its classification tests were nearly as powerful as a truncated SPRT's and considerably shorter | {cite:p}`Bartroff2008` | [glr-stopper.md](https://github.com/douglasrizzo/catsim/blob/dev/specs/stopping/glr-stopper.md) |
| Stochastic curtailment stopper | stopping | Stops a truncated SPRT once its classification is unlikely to change before the maximum test length, so it never administers more items than the SPRT; in Finkelman's simulation, it shortened the tests of examinees near the cut score with virtually no loss of accuracy | {cite:p}`Finkelman2008`; {cite:p}`Finkelman2010` | [stochastic-curtailment-stopper.md](https://github.com/douglasrizzo/catsim/blob/dev/specs/stopping/stochastic-curtailment-stopper.md) |
| Kullback-Leibler information stopper | stopping | Stops when no remaining item has enough integrated KL information around the ability estimate, so that stopping uses the same scale as KL item selection; no source proposes this rule, which applies the minimum-information rule to Chang and Ying's KL index | {cite:p}`Chang1996`; {cite:p}`Choi2011` | [kl-stopper.md](https://github.com/douglasrizzo/catsim/blob/dev/specs/stopping/kl-stopper.md) |
| Minimum information in bank stopper | stopping | Stops when no remaining item is informative enough at the ability estimate, which shortens the tests of examinees at extreme abilities, for whom the item bank has few informative items; alone it typically gives less precise estimates than a {doc}`standard error stopper </api/techniques/stopping/standard-error-stopper>`, and Choi et al. combine the two | {cite:p}`Dodd1989`; {cite:p}`Choi2011` | [min-info-bank-stopper.md](https://github.com/douglasrizzo/catsim/blob/dev/specs/stopping/min-info-bank-stopper.md) |
| Predicted standard error reduction stopper (PSER) | stopping | Predicts how much the best remaining item would reduce the standard error, stops before the target when that reduction is negligible, and continues past the target while it is still substantial | {cite:p}`Choi2011` | [predicted-sem-stopper.md](https://github.com/douglasrizzo/catsim/blob/dev/specs/stopping/predicted-sem-stopper.md) |
